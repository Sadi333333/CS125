DIGITAL SECURITY • INTERACTIVE

Security

This section breaks digital security into simple topics you can explore, then test yourself with a drag-and-drop quiz.

🚨

Watch Out For

Common tricks and threats that can put your information or devices at risk.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, texts, or websites that try to make you reveal passwords or personal information.

🕵️

Social Engineering

Someone manipulates you into giving access or information instead of attacking the technology directly.

🦠

Malware

Malicious software can damage devices, steal information, or give someone unwanted access.

⚠️

Suspicious Links

Unexpected links and attachments can lead to fake login pages, malware, or scams.

🚨

Unexpected Activity

Unknown logins, verification requests, or account changes can be signs that someone is trying to access an account.

🛡

Protect Yourself

Simple habits that make your accounts, devices, and information harder to compromise.

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords and avoid reusing the same password everywhere.

📱

Multi-Factor Authentication

Add another verification step so a stolen password alone is not enough to sign in.

⬆️

Keep Things Updated

Software updates often include security fixes that protect against known weaknesses.

👀

Think Before You Click

Check who sent something, inspect links, and slow down when a message creates urgency.

🔔

Watch Login Alerts

Pay attention to security notifications and verification requests so you can act quickly when something looks unfamiliar.

⚖

Online Ethics

Using technology responsibly means thinking about how your actions affect other people.

🧾

Copyright

Respect the creators of photos, videos, writing, software, and other digital work.

✍️

Plagiarism

Give credit for other people's work and make it clear when ideas or words are not your own.

🤝

Respect

Communicate responsibly online and remember that there are real people behind accounts and screens.

🤖

Responsible AI

Use AI honestly, check its output, and avoid using it to mislead or harm others.
